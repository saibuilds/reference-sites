# Build-Prompt Library — 15 archetypes

Every page in this repo (70 pages, 20 reels) is one of the 15 archetypes below.
To build a site for any business: pick the archetype whose look you want, copy its
prompt block, and replace only the **PROJECT / AUDIENCE / GOAL / SECTION COPY** —
keep BRAND, SECTIONS and ANIMATIONS to reproduce the proven look in any builder
(Lovable, Bolt, v0, Framer, or hand-coded HTML+GSAP).

The 7 layers every prompt below stacks: **Role → Brand → Structure → Content →
Animation → Tech → Output.** Swap content, never the structure, to stay on-style.

agency build, in the "Luxury Dark" style (reference feel: Cartier · Obsidian Dew · OF Sakazuki).

- Background: #0A0A0A   Surface: #161412
- Text: #F5F0E8   Accent: #C9A96E
- Heading font: Cormorant Garamond   Body font: Inter
- Tone: restrained, luxurious, quiet
- Reference styles: Cartier · Obsidian Dew · OF Sakazuki

SECTIONS (exact order, do not reorder):
1. Hero: centered product/brand with a soft glowing orb behind the headline
2. Story: large pull-quote beside a supporting paragraph
3. Offering: 3-up numbered cards of core products/services
4. Horizontal-scroll panels of materials/details
5. Footer: brand mark + email + social

- Smooth scroll via Lenis (always).

- Section reveals: hero glow + fade-up headline; reveal-on-scroll; staggered card reveal; horizontal scroll pin.
- Buttons: magnetic + subtle glow on the accent color.
- Page-load: brand mark reveal → fade to hero.
- Sticky nav that blurs/shrinks on scroll.

Do not invent testimonials, stats, or claims.

- Mobile-first responsive; <2s LCP; lazy-load media.
