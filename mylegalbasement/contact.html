— Asked Often —

Three questions, answered straight.

How long does a permit take in Toronto?
Eight to twelve weeks for a standard second-suite permit, longer if the lot needs a minor variance from the Committee of Adjustment. We draft the working set, file it, and respond to plan-examiner comments on your behalf.
What does a typical basement suite cost?
A clean, walk-out two-bedroom legal basement is $95K-$130K turnkey in 2026 dollars. Underpinning adds $40K-$70K. Garden suites are a separate conversation — usually $260K-$340K for 645 sqft.
Do you place the tenant?
Yes. Every project ends with a vetted lease in your hand. We photograph the finished suite, list it on the major rental boards, screen applicants, and hand you the signed agreement before the keys.
