— The Story of the Suite —

Malvern is a neighbourhood of immigrant families on 35-foot lots. The basements are where the second income lives. We make sure that income is legal, safe, and bright.

47 Hupfield Trail was a 1978 bungalow with an unfinished basement that the family had been using as storage for eleven years. The owner, a long-haul truck driver, and his wife, a hospital cleaner, wanted a legal two-bedroom suite that would help carry the mortgage and eventually house his sister's family when they arrived from Jaffna. The brief was simple: do it properly, do it once, do it so the city signs off and the insurance is clean.

The existing basement had a seven-foot ceiling at the lowest point, a stair that came down through the kitchen, and no separate entrance. The first move was to underpin the south wall by ten inches to bring the ceiling to a legal seven-foot-six clear, and to cut a new walk-down stair through the side yard with a window-well door at grade. Both moves required a Committee of Adjustment minor variance, which we secured in week six.

The interior is two bedrooms with egress windows, a 3-piece bath with linear drain, a galley kitchen with a 24-inch range, and a living-dining strip with a south-facing window that pulls light off the side-entrance walk-down. The flooring is luxury vinyl plank in a pale oak. The walls are painted Benjamin Moore Chantilly Lace, the trim is square stock, and the doors are flat slabs with brushed nickel handles. Nothing is precious; everything wipes clean.

The first tenant was a nurse and her partner who saw the suite the week before the city did the final inspection. They moved in on the first of the following month, on a one-year lease at $2,400, hydro included. The family upstairs got their first rent cheque on the 5th. The sister from Jaffna is still coming, but the timeline shifted. The income is keeping the mortgage on schedule in the meantime.
