Permits, in plain English

Four checkpoints between you and a legal suite.

Every step is something the City of Toronto will ask about. We do them in order, on your behalf, and your name only appears on the final inspection certificate.

01 · Zoning

Zoning check

Lot frontage, ceiling height, gross floor area — verified against the City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013 before we touch a drawing.

Typical 3 – 5 business days
02 · Drawings

Architect drawings

OBC-compliant floor plan, section, fire-separation detail, electrical layout. Stamped by a licensed Ontario architect or BCIN designer.

Typical 2 – 3 weeks
03 · Permit

City permit application

Submitted via the City of Toronto building permit portal. We answer reviewer comments, resubmit revisions, and track the file to issuance.

Typical 4 – 8 weeks
04 · Sign-off

Final inspection

Framing, insulation, electrical (ESA), plumbing, and final occupancy inspections — all closed out. You receive the City inspection record.

Typical 2 – 3 weeks
