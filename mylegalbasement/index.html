Your house already holds a second one.

I grew up in a Scarborough house where my parents quietly rented the basement to a graduate student named Ravi. That second income paid the mortgage faster than two adult salaries could. I watched a family build wealth from a hallway and a separate side door.

Twelve years later, that is the only job I do. I help Toronto families add a second home to the one they already own — underground or in the backyard — with permits filed, builders supervised, and a tenant placed before the dust settles. No subcontracted sales team. No franchise. Just me, a small crew of carpenters and electricians I have worked with for a decade, and a phone you can call on a Saturday.
