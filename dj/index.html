— The Method · Four Acts —

From bare studs to finished room.

I
Survey.
Laser tape, LiDAR scan, existing-conditions drawings. Joist directions, HVAC runs, panel capacity, plumbing stack. Documented before a single line is drawn. You keep the deliverables whether you build with us or not.
Week one · LiDAR · On-site 90 min
II
Spec.
3D model, finishes spec book, line-item budget. Cabinet pulls, tile patterns, panel schedules, hardware finishes — the entire room rendered, costed, and revised before you sign anything.
2–3 weeks · BCIN drawings · Fixed price
III
Build.
Cabinetry fabricated in our Etobicoke shop. Licensed electrical (ESA), plumbing, HVAC, tile in-house or vetted long-term subs. One project manager on site, one phone number, weekly photo updates, milestone-tied draws.
6–12 weeks · In-house shop · Milestone draws
IV
Handover.
Punch list closed. Keys, manuals, finish samples, leftover tile in labelled boxes. TARION where applicable, 5-year cabinetry warranty as standard. We come back at month 6, 12 and 24 to adjust, snug, and re-finish — in the contract, not the brochure.
24-month follow-up · TARION · 5-yr cabinetry

— Six Honest Answers —

Things people actually ask.

Q.01How long for a kitchen? ›
Typical full kitchen build is 8 to 12 weeks on site, plus 4 to 6 weeks in our cabinet shop running in parallel. Heritage homes or structural work (load-bearing wall removal, joist reinforcement) add 1 to 2 weeks. We commit to a hand-over date on the spec book and tie payment draws to milestones, not the calendar.
Q.02What is the cabinet lead time? ›
Custom cabinets are built in our Etobicoke shop. From signed spec book to delivery: 4 to 6 weeks for paint-grade and quarter-sawn species, 6 to 8 weeks for rift white oak and walnut, 8 to 10 weeks for inset doors with hand-fit drawer fronts. Hardware (Blum, Hafele) is stocked.
Q.03Is the permit included? ›
BCIN-stamped drawings, City of Toronto permit application, and plans-examination cycles are all included in the fixed-price contract for any project requiring permit (most basements, all garden suites, kitchens with structural or plumbing relocations). Permit fees are passed through at cost with the city receipt attached.
Q.04What warranty do you offer? ›
5 years on cabinetry, drawer slides, hinges and powder-coated hardware. 2 years on tile, grout, paint and trim. TARION-registered new-home warranty on garden suites (1-year workmanship, 2-year mechanical, 7-year structural). We come back at month 6, 12 and 24 to adjust hinges, snug screws and touch up paint — in the contract, not the brochure.
Q.05Can the garden suite be financed? ›
Yes. Most clients refinance against their existing home equity to fund a garden suite, then service the new debt with the rental income. We work with three mortgage brokers who specialise in CMHC Multi-Unit Mortgage Loan Insurance and the city of Toronto’s Second-Suite incentive programmes. We do introductions, not commissions.
Q.06Toronto vs GTA — same pricing? ›
Within Toronto and the inner 416 (Etobicoke, North York, Scarborough, East York) the price is flat. Mississauga, Vaughan, Markham and Richmond Hill carry a 4-to-6 percent travel premium to cover the second site supervisor we put on those projects. Beyond Highway 407 we quote case-by-case, usually only for repeat clients.
— A Letter From The Workshop —

Twelve years on tools. One contractor on your file.

I started swinging a hammer at nineteen, hung my first cabinet at twenty-one, and signed my first permit set at twenty-six. Twelve years on, DJ Custom Reno is the workshop I always wanted to work at — a small team of carpenters, electricians and tile-setters I have personally trained, working out of one Etobicoke cabinet shop, building one room at a time.

The brief is always the same. One contractor on the file. You meet me at site survey. I draw your spec book. I run your build. I close your punch list. When you call us about a sticky drawer in 2031, I am the person who picks up the phone.

If that is the kind of build you want, write me a line. We do site surveys on Tuesday and Thursday mornings, and the first ninety minutes — the laser tape, the LiDAR scan, the existing-conditions drawing — is yours to keep whether you go forward with us or not. The conversation is the gift. The relationship is the point.

— Sai Nathan · Master Fabricator · Toronto
