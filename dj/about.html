— The Founder —

Sai Nathan, atelier director.

I started swinging a hammer at nineteen, hung my first cabinet at twenty-one, and signed my first permit set at twenty-six. By twenty-eight I was running site for a midtown general contractor and watching the same problem on every file: too many hands, no one in charge of the work, a homeowner left chasing four different phone numbers when the kitchen drawer stopped closing.

DJ Custom Reno opened in 2019 to fix exactly that. We are a small shop in Etobicoke. Six bench carpenters, two finish carpenters, a tile setter, an electrician, and a draftsperson who also runs scheduling. We draw our own spec books, fabricate our own casework, install our own stone partners' material, and close our own punch lists. The same hands that lay out your site survey on a Tuesday morning are the hands that hang your last brass pull on a Friday afternoon ten weeks later.

The brief is always the same: one contractor on the file. You meet me at the survey. I draw the spec book. I run the build. I close the punch list. When you call us about a sticky drawer in 2031, I am the person who picks up the phone. There is no franchise. There is no second crew working a second job across town. We do about twelve full kitchens a year and another fifteen or so smaller fit-outs — pantries, mudrooms, lower-level bars, garden-suite kitchens for our sister brand.

I live in Etobicoke with my partner and a one-eyed shop cat named Belt-Sander. I drive a 2014 Sierra with two hundred and forty thousand kilometres on it. I believe a kitchen, built right, is the load-bearing piece of furniture in a family's life. We build them like contractors, finish them like cabinetmakers, and stand behind them like the people who made them, because we are.

— What We Stand For —

Four shop rules, in plain English.

I
One contractor on the file.
You meet me at survey. I run the build. I sign the punch list. No project manager you have never met. No subcontracted sales team. One number, one builder, ten years.
II
Draw it, then build it.
Every job ships with a spec book: cabinet elevations, hardware schedule, stone shop drawings, electrical legend. If it is not on paper, we do not build it.
III
Build it like it is ours.
Dovetailed drawer boxes, soft-close on every door, edge-banded interiors, full-extension blum on every slide. The boring stuff that costs us margin and earns you a decade.
IV
Pick up the phone in 2031.
The relationship is the warranty. When you call about a sticky drawer or a refinish in seven years, the person who picks up is the person who built it.
