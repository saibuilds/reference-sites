— Asked Often —

Three questions, plain answers.

How long does a full kitchen take, start to finish?
Eight weeks shop time, three weeks on site, one week of punch. From signed contract to handed-over keys, plan on twelve to fourteen weeks for a standard 18-cabinet kitchen with stone. Full renos with structural work run 18-24 weeks.
What does a custom kitchen actually cost?
A clean 18-cabinet kitchen in painted maple with a single-slab quartz top, full appliance install, and tile backsplash is $95K-$140K turnkey in 2026 dollars. Walnut or rift-white-oak adds 18-25 percent. Stone island upgrades add another $8K-$22K.
Do you handle plumbing, electrical, and tile?
Yes. Our crew runs cabinetry, finish carpentry, and tile in-house. Our electrician and plumber have been on our files for six and nine years respectively. One number, one schedule, one punch list. You do not chase trades.
