— Questions, Answered —

Three things asked often.

Do you take buyers under $1M?
Yes — selectively. Our roster is capped at sixty-five families, so we accept first-time buyers when the relationship feels right and the search is realistic. We will tell you on the first call.
What neighbourhoods do you focus on?
Forest Hill, Rosedale, Lawrence Park, the Bridle Path, Yorkville, and a handful of midtown blocks. We walk the neighbourhoods twice a week and know the laneways from St. Clair to Eglinton.
How do you charge?
Standard buy- and sell-side commission on transactions. Advisory and off-market mandates are billed quarterly, on an open retainer agreed at the kitchen-table meeting.
